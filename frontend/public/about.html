About

Support Without The Waiting Game

NO-Q is a hybrid AI ticketing SaaS for companies who want clean triage, clear ownership, and a branded customer support flow.

View Pricing Try Live Support

The Problem

Customers explain issues in messy, unstructured ways. Teams lose time extracting details, deciding priority, and routing the right work.

The Method

A branded assistant captures the context. AI + policies help triage. A structured ticket is created every time, with a clean transcript.

The Outcome

Faster triage, clearer queues, fewer back-and-forths, and reporting that shows what issues are happening most.

What NO-Q Is Built For

Multi-tenant workspaces that feel like real SaaS, not a demo.

Tenant Isolation

Tickets, configs, branding, and KB entries are scoped by company.

Supervisor vs Agent

Supervisors manage the workspace; agents operate the ticket inbox.

Hybrid AI

RAG + policies keeps support answers relevant and consistent.
